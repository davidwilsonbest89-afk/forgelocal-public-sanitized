# Code of Conduct

BrowseForge is an international open-source project. Contributors and users are expected to communicate professionally and focus on technical substance.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful across language, culture, experience level, and timezone.
- Assume good intent, but correct inaccurate technical claims with evidence.
- Keep discussions focused on reproducible behavior, design tradeoffs, and project goals.
- Accept maintainer decisions when they are made to protect stability, safety, or scope.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information, secrets, tokens, cookies, or profile data.
- Encouraging abuse, unauthorized access, spam, fraud, or credential misuse.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests after maintainers ask to refocus.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or hide comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants when behavior harms the project or community. Security-sensitive or abuse-enabling content may be removed without extended discussion.
